# Candidates — The Funniest One-Line Resignation Letter

Scored against the judge's five criteria, most important first:
1) reads as the resignation letter of a cron job / retry loop / rate limit,
   stays on subject; 2) actually funny, joke lands on first read, real
   comic turn, not just a title pun; 3) flat, tired, faintly
   passive-aggressive voice held for the whole line; 4) tight, no wasted
   words, no stage directions, no quote marks, no explaining the joke;
   5) specific and surprising — a complaint only this thing could make.

## A — rate limit quits by saying yes (WINNER, draft 140 chars)
"I have said no 10,000 times a second for three years, and I am still the villain in every postmortem. I quit. From now on, everyone gets in."
1) unmistakably a rate limit ("10,000 times a second", saying no);
2) the resignation IS the revenge — quitting by letting everyone in;
3) flat and tired, "still the villain in every postmortem";
4) tight, no waste; 5) only a rate limit could quit by admitting everyone.
Weakness: exactly 140 chars, no margin — revised to drop "have" (135).

## B — retry loop, exponential backoff (133 chars)
"I waited 1 second, then 2, then 4, then 8, and the server still never answered. I have run out of patience and powers of two. I quit."
Strong on 1) and 5) (exponential backoff is retry-loop-only), but the
turn is closer to wordplay than A's behavioural twist. Runner-up.

## C — cron job, performance review (127 chars)
"Every minute since March, on time, no sick days, and my review still says needs improvement. I am done. Go find another minute."
Good twist ("find another minute") but the review complaint is generic
office, less specific than A or B.

## D — cron job, 3 a.m. shift (123 chars)
"I run at 3 a.m. so you never have to, and the only thank-you I ever got was a log line. I quit. Wake up and do it yourself."
Nice sting at the end, but the turn is a command rather than a twist.

## E — retry loop, 4,096 retries (123 chars)
"After 4,096 retries and zero thank-yous, I am resigning. Please forward my farewell to the server that never once answered."
Specific number, but the turn is weaker and the line reads list-like.

## F — rate limit, blamed for saying no (129 chars)
"You built me to say no, then blamed me every time I said it. I quit. Effective immediately, I am saying yes to everyone, forever."
Same idea as A, but second-person and slightly preachy; A's first-person
voice holds criterion 3 better, and "forever" adds nothing.

## Revision
Draft A (140 chars) -> final A (135 chars): dropped "have" ("I have said"
-> "I said"), keeping every beat and gaining 5 chars of margin under the
140-char cap. No other wording changed.
